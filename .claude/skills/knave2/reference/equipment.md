# Equipment (pp. 38–43)

## Coinage and prices (p. 38)

- Everything is priced in **coins (c)**. There are no copper/silver/gold denominations. **10c = one day of unskilled labor**; price anything unusual by asking how many days of work it is worth.
- **500 coins fill one item slot.**
- Converting from other old-school games (p. 74): **1 gold piece = 1c**, so Knave PCs get rich slowly; big purchases come from trading unique treasures to rich NPCs, and favors go further than coin.
- **Rarity tiers:** **common** 5c, any settlement (rope, torch, saw, arrow, quiver); **uncommon** 20c, towns and cities (lantern, bear trap); **rare** 100c+, cities only (sextant, hourglass, astrolabe).

Weapon type is cosmetic (see the weapons table for 100 names) except where a monster's weakness or immunity names a damage type (piercing, blunt, cutting, silver, fire…). Weapons break on a natural 1 and on power attacks, so fighters carry several.

**Armor pieces** — up to **7** worn, each **1 slot** and **+1 AP**; max **7 AP = AC 18**: Shield 100c · Helmet 100c · Gambeson 100c · Mail shirt 200c · Breastplate 500c · Arm plate 500c · Leg plate 500c. Armor is tailored to the wearer, so resale value is negligible.
