# Society: buildings, warfare, downtime, recruiting, NPCs (pp. 44–59)

## Buildings (p. 44)

- **Businesses and investments** return about **d10 % of their cost per year** if all goes to plan.
- **Renting:** common room 2c/night · private room 10c/night · a whole building 1 % of its value per month.
- **Buying** (approximate; condition, size, and importance move the price; sum several to price a custom building):

| Building | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Poor hovel | 100c |
| Humble cottage | 1,000c |
| Respectable house, chapel | 10,000c |
| Wealthy house, guildhall, theater, warehouse | 50,000c |
| Church, stone tower | 100,000c |
| Small castle | 2,000,000c |
| Minor noble's mansion, temple, medium castle | 8,000,000c |
| Large castle | 32,000,000c |
| Major noble's estate, cathedral | 100,000,000c |
| Imperial palace, basilica | 400,000,000c |

- **Construction**, cost per **5′ square** of floor (10′ ceilings assumed). Staffing a non-business building costs **50 % of its cost per year**. An ambitious PC can use this to build a dungeon.

| Style | Materials | Per 5′ square |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Poor | Earth walls, thatch, earth floors | 10c |
| Humble | Wattle and daub with some brick or field stone, thatch, reed floors | 50c |
| Respectable | Wood and brick with some stone, shingles, wood floors | 250c |
| Wealthy | Mostly stone with some wood and brick, shingles, wood floors — also the cost of a 5′ cube of defensive stone wall | 1,000c |
| Minor noble / castle | Stone walls, shingles, flagstone floors, some glass and marble, minor flourishes | 5,000c |
| Major noble | Stone walls and roof, tiled floors, glass windows, high ceilings, significant flourishes | 50,000c |
| Royal | Marble walls and floors, stone roof, glass, soaring ceilings, art everywhere | 100,000c |

## Warfare (p. 45)

For battles (or warband skirmishes) too big to play out with the combat rules.

**Units.** Each of the following costs **100,000c per month** and has **equal fighting power**: 1 master battle wizard · 4 battle wizards · 25 elite cavalry · 50 veteran cavalry · 100 trained cavalry · 200 untrained cavalry · 50 elite foot or archers · 100 veteran foot or archers · 200 trained foot or archers · 400 untrained foot or archers.

1. **Tally fighting power** per side. GM may adjust a side by up to ±50 % for morale and position. Each leader may make a **CHA check** to add 50 % with a stirring speech. Relative strength is what matters.
2. **Establish bonus** for the stronger side: up to 50 % stronger +2 · up to 2× +4 · up to 3× +6 · up to 4× +8 · more than 4× +10.
3. **Battle checks.** The stronger side makes **three checks** with that bonus vs **11**.
4. **Pick results.** Each success lets the stronger side secretly pick one result; each failure lets the weaker side pick one. Repeats allowed. Reveal and resolve together: **Capture** (an enemy NPC is captured) · **Loot** (an enemy item is looted) · **Slay** (10 % of the enemy force dies) · **Rescue** (cancels a Capture) · **Guard** (cancels a Loot) · **Shield** (cancels a Slay). The side with **more picks** wins; the other withdraws.

## Downtime (p. 52)

- **Carousing.** A whole night; costs **d10 × 50c** in a village, **d10 × 100c** in a town, **d10 × 200c** in a city, and grants **XP equal to the coins spent**. Next morning pass a **CON check** or roll a mishap:

| d20 | Carousing mishap |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | You made a public fool of yourself |
| 2 | Take d3 direct damage from a fight |
| 3 | Pay d100c in fines |
| 4 | You are engaged to be married |
| 5 | Lose d1000c gambling |
| 6 | Groupies follow you everywhere |
| 7 | You've made an enemy |
| 8 | You have an ugly, prominent tattoo |
| 9 | Hangover: −5 on all checks today |
| 10 | You joined a local faction (p. 50) |
| 11 | Robbed: lose all remaining coin |
| 12 | You wake up in prison |
| 13 | The building is on fire! |
| 14 | You're expected to complete a mission (p. 51) because of your boasts |
| 15 | A duel is scheduled for the next dawn |
| 16 | You signed a shady contract |
| 17 | A stranger's corpse is on the floor |
| 18 | A faction hates you (p. 50) |
| 19 | All your belongings have been stolen |
| 20 | A new companion wants to join the party |

- **Gambling.** Wager up to the house limit; GM rolls d6. Player then either **bows out** and forfeits **half** the wager, or rolls a d6 trying to **beat** the GM's roll: success **doubles** the wager, failure loses **all** of it.
- **Career training** — see `careers.md`.

## Recruiting (p. 53)

All hired help has **10 item slots**. Availability rerolls monthly.

| Type | Monthly | Stats | Behaviour | Available |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hirelings** (porters, torchbearers) | 300c | AC 11, HP 3, LVL 1, ATK punch (d2), MOV 40′, MRL 4 | Unskilled labor; avoid danger at all costs, run at the first sign of trouble | village d10 · town 3d10 · city 10d10 |
| **Mercenaries** (swordsmen, archers) | 600c | AC 15, HP 3, LVL 1, ATK weapon (d6), MOV 40′, MRL 8 | Fight for the PCs; **will not enter dungeons** | village d6 · town 3d6 · city 10d6 |
| **Experts** | common 600c · uncommon 1,200c · rare 2,400c | AC 11, HP 3, LVL 1, ATK punch (d2), MOV 40′, MRL 7 | Only their specialty; follow into dungeons but neither fight nor take risks | common: anywhere · uncommon: towns and cities · rare: cities |
| **Companions** | share of loot | Generated like a PC | No morale checks, fight to the death; take a **half share** of treasure and XP; gain levels; follow only PCs of **higher level** | lifetime max per PC = **CHA** |
