# Careers (p. 5)

Careers replace classes and backgrounds. Each PC starts with **two**, rolled or picked. A career gives:

- its three listed **items** at creation (slot cost follows the normal rules — one slot each, two for two-handed items, small groups share a slot);
- **career knowledge** — the PC simply knows what a practitioner would know, no roll;
- permission to **attempt related tasks** that would otherwise be impossible without training;
- **+5** on related **non-combat** checks. Careers never add to attacks or maneuvers.

Starting kit on top of both careers' items, as far as slots allow: `3d6 × 10` coins, 2 rations, 50′ rope, 2 torches, any armor pieces and weapons wanted from the equipment list, a quiver of 20 arrows, and one random spellbook per point of INT.

## Career training (p. 52)

New careers are learned from an expert in the field for time and money. Three tiers:

| Tier | To attempt related tasks | To also gain the +5 | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 1 month, 1,000c (grants both at once) | — | carpenter, hunter, fisherman, sailor, dyer, gardener, blacksmith |
| Uncommon | 3 months, 5,000c | +3 months, +5,000c | burglar, acrobat, locksmith, grave robber, herbalist, tattooist, prospector |
| Rare | 1 year, 30,000c | +1 year, +30,000c | alchemist, lawyer, assassin, sculptor, folklorist |
